ABOUT KEMILAU GEMA INDOPACK

Reliable Solutions Partner of Primary Packaging

Kemilau Gema Indopack was founded in 2025 on the clear vision to become a reliable solutions partner of primary packaging for pharmaceutical, biotechnology, cosmetic, veterinary, food, and beverages.

Our Heritage & Group Strength

Backed by Over 19 Years of Industry Dedication

We are a newly formed business as part of the KGI Group — Kinas Global Indonusa — with over 19 years of dedicated experience (since 2007) as a premier raw materials supplier for the Indonesian market.

Combining KGI Group's deep supply chain foundation, established local warehousing, and robust procurement network, Kemilau Gema Indopack delivers world-class primary packaging that meets the highest standards of product protection, chemical compatibility, and regulatory safety.
